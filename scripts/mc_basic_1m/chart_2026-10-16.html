Trades are numbered from #0, as in the report text. W means Net $ above zero. Net $ is after $3.00 fees per round trip. The entry fills 1 tick worse than the next bar's open, and a stop fills 1 tick worse than the stop price (or than the open, when the bar opens past the stop). Gross $ runs from the entry bar's open to the exit before slippage. That exit is the stop or target price, or the exit bar's open when that bar opens past the stop or target.
