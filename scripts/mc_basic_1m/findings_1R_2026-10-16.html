Why 1:1 still loses (HTF-a)

  • 103 of 149 stops sat on the 2-point floor, so most targets are only 2 points away.
  • On those floor trades a win nets +$7.00 in 38 of 39 cases (a gap open fills at a different price): 2 points is $10.00, less $3.00 fees. A loss costs −$14.25 in 63 of 64 cases (a gap open fills at a different price): the stop fills 1 tick worse, so 2.25 points is $11.25, plus $3.00 fees.
  • Average win +$8.07, average loss −$15.51. Break-even needs a 65.8% win rate. Actual: 36.9% (Wilson 95%: 29.6% to 44.9%).
  • Gross P&L before fees and slippage is −$263.75, so 1:1 also loses before costs. Costs add −$750.75.
  • Largest win +$37.00, largest loss −$36.75.
  • Funnel: 446 triggers = 5 ignored while in a position + 292 failed the 15m filter + 0 had no target + 149 closed. The SR run dropped 2 triggers for having no target.

  • The official TradingView MCP returned no 1m bars before 2026-10-11 ("available history starts 2026-10-11").
  • 15m history goes back to 2026-04-30 (11,056 bars), but triggers, stops and exits all run on 1m bars, so 15m alone cannot replace it.

  • A 3-month window would also cross the September contract roll (Sep 5 to 20 ET), which the simulator refuses by design.

Rules tested

  • Long trigger: MarketCipher_B Buy dot. Short trigger: Blue Wave Crossing Down with Blue Wave at or above 60.
  • Entry at the next bar's open plus or minus 1 tick.
  • 15m filter: HTF-a needs Money Flow rising, HTF-b needs 2 of 3 rising (Money Flow, Blue Wave, RSI), HTF-c needs all 3. Shorts use falling.
  • Stop 1 tick below the setup bar's low for longs, 1 tick above its high for shorts, clamped to 2 to 8 points. Call that distance D.
  • Target at entry plus D for longs, entry minus D for shorts (1:1). Cipher Resistance and Support are not used. The target fills only when price trades 1 tick through it.
  • Costs: $5 per point, $3 fees per trade, 1 tick slippage.
