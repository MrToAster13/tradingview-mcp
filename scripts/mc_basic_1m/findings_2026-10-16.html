Rules tested

  • Long trigger: MarketCipher_B Buy dot. Short trigger: Blue Wave Crossing Down with Blue Wave at or above 60.
  • Entry at the next bar's open plus or minus 1 tick.
  • 15m filter: HTF-a needs Money Flow rising, HTF-b needs 2 of 3 rising (Money Flow, Blue Wave, RSI), HTF-c needs all 3. Shorts use falling.
  • Stop 1 tick below the setup bar's low for longs, 1 tick above its high for shorts, clamped to 2 to 8 points.
  • Target at the nearest confirmed Cipher Resistance (longs) or Support (shorts). The SR indicator runs on a 5m setting, so levels come from the 5m bar 3 bars back. This rule matches 80 of 80 Resistance and 87 of 87 Support changes in the data.
  • Costs: $5 per point, $3 fees per trade, 1 tick slippage.

Fixes made during the audits

  1. Target levels. The SR levels follow the indicator's 5m setting and count only 5m bars that exist, so the daily halt and the weekend do not shift them. Counting 15 clock minutes back instead matched only 85 of 87 Support changes.
  2. Setting check. The simulator only accepts an SR setting that is a whole number of minutes and divides an hour.
  3. Snapshot. The snapshot now records the SR setting, and the forward test refuses two snapshots taken with different settings.
  4. Chart-reading crash. The read script crashed on drawings ("s.metaInfo is not a function"). This bug predates the work.
  5. Typo in the setting check. A literal d where \d was meant rejected every setting of 10 minutes or more. Fixed, with a test that fails on the old code.
  6. Design note. The level counts in the design note were stale. Corrected to the measured values.

A later audit found that the stop rule above described shorts wrongly; it now says 1 tick above the high. Tests after the 1:1 work: 377 run, 369 pass, 7 fail, 1 skipped. All 7 failures are in lint.test.mjs and predate this work.

Where the plan changed

  • TradingView was relaunched after the power loss.
  • Data came from hidden chart sessions instead of a second tab. The visible chart was only read, never changed.
  • 1m history starts Oct 4, the furthest back the chart loaded.
  • The official TradingView MCP was added as mcp-tradingview. There was no old one to remove.
  • The target rule was changed to the 5m setting because the original rule matched only 1 of 80 levels.
  • The SR setting was added to the existing snapshot by hand, from a live read of the indicator.
